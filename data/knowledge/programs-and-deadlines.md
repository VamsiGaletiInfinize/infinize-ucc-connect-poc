# Programmes offered

## Undergraduate

- **B.Tech Computer Science and Engineering** — 4 years, 180 seats
- **B.Tech Electronics and Communication** — 4 years, 120 seats
- **B.Tech Mechanical Engineering** — 4 years, 90 seats
- **B.Sc Data Science** — 3 years, 60 seats
- **BBA Business Administration** — 3 years, 90 seats

## Postgraduate

- **M.Tech Computer Science** — 2 years, 40 seats. Specialisations in Artificial
  Intelligence, Cyber Security and Distributed Systems.
- **M.Tech VLSI Design** — 2 years, 30 seats
- **MBA** — 2 years, 120 seats. Specialisations in Business Analytics, Finance, Marketing
  and Operations.
- **M.Sc Applied Mathematics** — 2 years, 40 seats

## Doctoral

- **Ph.D. in Computer Science, Electronics, Mathematics and Management** — 3 to 5 years,
  admission twice yearly.

## Late submission

Applications received after the deadline are considered only if seats remain and attract a
late fee of ₹2,000. Documents received after the document submission deadline are not
accepted, and the application lapses. A lapsed application cannot be reinstated in the same
intake.

## Deferral

An admitted candidate may request a one-time deferral to the next equivalent intake, in
writing, within 14 days of the offer. Deferral is granted at the discretion of the
department and requires the seat acceptance fee to have been paid.
