# Applicant portal support

## Signing in

The applicant portal is at https://apply.infinize.edu. Sign in with the email address used
at registration. If you have forgotten your password, use the "Forgot password" link; a
reset link is sent to your registered email and is valid for 30 minutes.

Accounts are locked for 15 minutes after five consecutive failed sign-in attempts. The
lock clears automatically; there is no need to contact support.

## Document upload problems

Uploads must be PDF files under 5 MB. The most common causes of a failed upload are:

- A file larger than 5 MB. Compress the PDF or split it into parts.
- A scanned image saved as JPG or PNG. Convert to PDF before uploading.
- A password-protected PDF. Remove the password before uploading.
- An unstable connection. The portal does not resume interrupted uploads; retry from the
  start.

An uploaded document shows as "Received" within a few minutes. If it still shows "Pending"
after 24 hours, contact technical support.

## Payment problems

If a payment is debited but the portal still shows the fee as unpaid, wait two hours before
retrying — most gateway confirmations settle automatically. If the status has not updated
after two hours, contact technical support with the bank reference number. Duplicate
payments are refunded to the original payment method within 7 to 10 working days.

## Browser support

The portal supports current versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari. Private browsing
and aggressive ad-blocking extensions can prevent document uploads.

## What we cannot discuss without verifying your identity

To protect applicants and students, the university does not disclose the following over the
telephone or by email until identity has been verified:

- Application status or admission decisions
- Documents received or outstanding on a specific application
- Fee balances, payment history or scholarship outcomes
- Examination results or academic records
- Personal contact details held on record

Identity verification uses a one-time passcode sent to the mobile number registered on the
application. General information about programmes, fees, deadlines and campus facilities is
public and does not require verification.
