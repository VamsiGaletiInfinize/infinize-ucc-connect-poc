# Admissions at Infinize University

Infinize University admits students to undergraduate, postgraduate and doctoral programmes
for the Autumn and Spring intakes. All applications are submitted through the applicant
portal at https://apply.infinize.edu. Paper applications are not accepted.

## How to apply

1. Create an applicant account using a valid email address and mobile number.
2. Complete the personal and academic details sections.
3. Select up to three programmes. Each programme selection is treated as a separate
   application with its own application number in the format `APP-YYYY-NNN`.
4. Upload the required documents in PDF format, each under 5 MB.
5. Pay the non-refundable application fee.
6. Submit. You will receive an application number by email and SMS immediately.

An application is only considered for review once it has been submitted and the fee has
been received. Saved drafts are not reviewed.

## Required documents

All applicants must provide:

- Class X and Class XII marksheets
- Transfer certificate from the last institution attended
- Government-issued photo identification (Aadhaar, passport or driving licence)
- Passport-size photograph taken within the last six months
- Category certificate, if claiming a reserved category fee or seat

Undergraduate applicants must additionally provide their Class XII final marksheet or, if
results are awaited, a provisional result statement.

Postgraduate applicants must additionally provide:

- Bachelor degree certificate and consolidated marksheet
- A valid entrance scorecard: GATE for M.Tech, CAT or XAT for MBA
- Statement of purpose of no more than 1,000 words
- Two letters of recommendation for research-track programmes
- Work experience letters, where the programme requires prior experience

Doctoral applicants must provide a research proposal of 2,000 to 3,000 words and evidence
of a qualifying master's degree.

## Application fees

The application fee is ₹1,200 per programme for Indian nationals and ₹3,500 for
international applicants. Applicants holding a valid category certificate pay a reduced
fee of ₹600. The fee is non-refundable once an application is submitted, including where
an application is later withdrawn.

## Eligibility

- **Undergraduate:** minimum 60% aggregate in Class XII, with at least 65% in the relevant
  subject stream. Engineering programmes require Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics.
- **Postgraduate:** minimum 60% aggregate in the qualifying bachelor degree, or an
  equivalent CGPA of 6.5 on a 10-point scale.
- **Doctoral:** a master's degree with a minimum 65% aggregate and a qualifying score in
  UGC-NET, CSIR-NET, GATE or the Infinize University Research Aptitude Test.

Candidates awaiting final results may apply provisionally. Admission remains conditional
until final marksheets are submitted.

## Selection process

Applications are reviewed by the departmental admissions committee. Shortlisted candidates
for postgraduate and doctoral programmes are invited to an online interview. Interview
invitations are sent at least seven days in advance to the email address on the
application.

Decisions are published on the applicant portal and communicated by email. Infinize
University does not disclose admission decisions over the telephone without identity
verification.

## Application status values

An application will show one of the following states on the portal:

- **DRAFT** — started but not yet submitted; not under consideration
- **SUBMITTED** — received and awaiting initial screening
- **UNDER_REVIEW** — with the departmental admissions committee
- **DOCUMENTS_PENDING** — one or more required documents are missing
- **INTERVIEW_SCHEDULED** — an interview has been arranged
- **ADMITTED** — an offer has been made
- **WAITLISTED** — held pending seat availability
- **REJECTED** — not selected for this intake
- **WITHDRAWN** — withdrawn at the applicant's request
