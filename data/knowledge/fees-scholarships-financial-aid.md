# Tuition fees for 2026-27

Fees are quoted per academic year and are payable in two instalments unless an instalment
plan has been approved.

| Programme | Annual tuition | Other charges |
|---|---|---|
| B.Tech (all branches) | ₹2,40,000 | ₹18,000 |
| B.Sc Data Science | ₹1,60,000 | ₹14,000 |
| BBA | ₹1,50,000 | ₹14,000 |
| M.Tech (all specialisations) | ₹2,10,000 | ₹16,000 |
| MBA | ₹3,70,000 | ₹22,000 |
| M.Sc Applied Mathematics | ₹1,40,000 | ₹12,000 |
| Ph.D. | ₹95,000 | ₹10,000 |

Other charges cover examination, library, laboratory and student activity fees. Hostel and
transport are charged separately.

## Payment schedule

- **Seat acceptance fee:** ₹25,000, due within 14 days of an offer and by 30 August 2026 at
  the latest. This amount is adjusted against the first instalment.
- **First instalment:** 60% of annual tuition, due before the semester begins.
- **Second instalment:** 40% of annual tuition, due by 15 January.

A late payment surcharge of 1.5% per month applies to overdue amounts. Students with
outstanding fees beyond 60 days may not register for examinations.

## Payment methods

Payment is accepted by net banking, UPI, debit card, credit card and NEFT/RTGS through the
student portal. Cash is not accepted at any campus office. Receipts are issued
electronically within 24 hours.

# Scholarships

## Merit scholarship

Awarded on entrance performance. Covers 25%, 50% or 100% of tuition depending on rank
band. No separate application is required — all admitted candidates are considered
automatically. Renewal each year requires a CGPA of 8.0 or above.

## Need-based financial aid

Available to students with an annual family income below ₹8,00,000. Covers up to 40% of
tuition. Requires an income certificate issued within the last twelve months, the previous
financial year's income tax return or Form 16, and a completed aid application.

## Sports and cultural scholarship

Covers up to 30% of tuition for students representing the state or nation in a recognised
sport or cultural discipline. Requires certification from the relevant authority.

## Research assistantship

Doctoral candidates receive a monthly stipend of ₹31,000 for the first two years and
₹35,000 thereafter, subject to satisfactory annual review.

## Scholarship application timeline

Scholarship applications for the Autumn 2026 intake close on **31 August 2026**. Decisions
are published by 10 September 2026. A scholarship decision is separate from an admission
decision and does not affect it.

# Education loans

Infinize University has arrangements with several scheduled banks. The Financial Aid Office
issues the fee structure letter and bona fide certificate required for a loan application,
normally within three working days of request. The university does not act as a guarantor
and does not process loan applications on a student's behalf.

## Fee waivers and hardship

Students facing documented financial hardship may apply to the Financial Aid Office for a
partial waiver or a revised instalment plan. Applications are reviewed monthly by the
hardship committee.
