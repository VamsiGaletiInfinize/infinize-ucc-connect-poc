# Campus

Infinize University occupies a 62-acre campus in Whitefield, Bengaluru. The campus houses
six academic blocks, a central library, an innovation centre, sports facilities and
residential accommodation.

The nearest metro station is Whitefield (Kadugodi) on the Purple Line, approximately
3 kilometres away. University shuttles run between the campus and the metro station every
20 minutes from 07:00 to 21:00.

## Library

The Central Library holds over 120,000 volumes and provides access to IEEE, ACM, JSTOR,
Scopus and ProQuest. Opening hours are 08:00 to 22:00 on weekdays and 09:00 to 18:00 on
Saturdays. During examination periods the library remains open until midnight.

## Laboratories and facilities

Specialist facilities include a high-performance computing cluster, a VLSI design
laboratory, an IoT and robotics workshop, a financial markets laboratory and a media
production studio. Laboratory access outside scheduled hours requires faculty approval.

# Hostel accommodation

On-campus accommodation is available in separate residences, allocated on a first-come
first-served basis after admission is confirmed. Accommodation is not guaranteed and
applications should be submitted as early as possible.

| Room type | Annual charge | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| Single occupancy, air-conditioned | ₹1,45,000 | Room, mess, laundry, Wi-Fi |
| Double occupancy, air-conditioned | ₹1,10,000 | Room, mess, laundry, Wi-Fi |
| Triple occupancy, non air-conditioned | ₹85,000 | Room, mess, laundry, Wi-Fi |

The mess provides vegetarian and non-vegetarian options at every meal, with dedicated
provision for Jain and vegan diets on request. Hostel residents must observe the 22:30
entry deadline unless a late pass has been issued.

## Applying for a hostel place

Hostel applications open once the seat acceptance fee has been paid and close on
5 September 2026 for the Autumn intake. Allocation is published on the student portal by
8 September 2026. A refundable security deposit of ₹15,000 is payable at allocation.

# Student services

## Health centre

An on-campus health centre is staffed by a physician and two nurses from 08:00 to 20:00 on
weekdays. A 24-hour ambulance service is available, and the university has a tie-up with a
multi-speciality hospital 4 kilometres from campus.

## Counselling

Confidential counselling is available free of charge to all enrolled students. Appointments
can be booked through the student portal or by walking in to the Student Wellbeing Centre
between 10:00 and 17:00.

## Career services

The Career Development Centre runs placement preparation, internship placement and employer
engagement. Over 240 organisations recruited from campus in the 2025-26 cycle.

## Transport

University buses operate on 14 routes across Bengaluru. The annual transport charge ranges
from ₹28,000 to ₹46,000 depending on route distance.

## Accessibility

All academic blocks, the library and two residences are wheelchair accessible. Students
requiring specific accommodations should contact the Office of Student Affairs at least two
weeks before the semester begins.
